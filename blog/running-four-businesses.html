Behind the Brand

Running Four Businesses Without Losing the Thread

How the lanes stay separate — and why they still feel like one story.

According to Jess

People ask me all the time how I keep So GorJess, GorJess Studios, J&J Imprint Co., and Dean Design Studio straight — like they expect the answer to be some elaborate system. Honestly, it's simpler than that. Each one has its own room. Its own brand, its own systems, its own identity. I don't try to make them all look like me.

What connects them isn't the visual branding. It's the standard. Whether it's a wig install, a suite rental agreement, a printed shirt, or a logo file — it has to meet the same bar, or it doesn't go out.

Different work. Same woman behind it.

That's become more than a tagline for me. It's genuinely how I operate. I'm not trying to be a "jack of all trades." I'm building a portfolio, on purpose, one lane at a time.
